- Website: [prylski.dev](https://prylski.dev/)

This attribution records the project concept and mathematical/visual synthesis,
consistent with the existing READMEs and `CITATION.cff`. It does not claim
authorship of established mathematical constants, prior mathematical results,
rainbow optics or third-party software.

The project distinguishes:

1. **Mathematics requiring verification:** geometry, selected comparison
   formulas, their numerical errors, the Golden Egg section and evaluation scores.
2. **The author's hypothesis about the construction's function:** an interpretation
   to be stated and confirmed by Michał Przybylski, with supporting evidence
   identified separately. The reviewed sources do not provide its precise wording.
3. **Artistic and schematic visualizations:** the rendered scene and its illustrative
   choices, which cannot establish a physical function or historical intention.

Existing acknowledgements to Philip Laven, Rich Jarvis and Alan Green record
references or inspiration. They do not transfer project authorship or imply
endorsement of the author's hypothesis. Refer to the existing README and
technical documents for the stated context of those acknowledgements.
