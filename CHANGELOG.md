## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-01

Interactive web laboratory (this repository).

- Parametric 11:7 pyramid in the browser (scale 1:3200, cubit 523.8 mm).
- Live comparison of mathematical constants as the slope angle changes.
- Primary and secondary rainbow correspondence in vertical XZ planes.
- Cross-eye stereoscopic 3D, reverse-depth toggle, fullscreen scene.
- English / Polish UI.
- **Golden Φ Egg** model: Harthun–Rennert cone `z = 1/r`, `Z₀ = 7.65`,
  solved `L/W = φ` → `αp = 51.795319256°`. Hyperbolic cone, planar
  section, and solid of revolution about the apothem.
- Constant row **L/W** (egg length/width vs φ).
- Source workbooks in `data/`.

Predecessor CAD model: [MichaelZP/great-pyramid-11-7](https://github.com/MichaelZP/great-pyramid-11-7) (Fusion 360, v1.7).
